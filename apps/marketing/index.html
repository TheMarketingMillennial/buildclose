How it works

Four steps from sent to signed.

BuildClose sits on top of the proposal you already deliver. It does not replace your document, your process, or your judgement about what can be offered.

  1. Bring your proposal

    Add BuildClose to an existing proposal document. The commercial facts the assistant may quote come from that document, so there is nothing separate to keep in sync.

  2. Set the boundaries

    You define what can be discussed and what cannot. Your internal floor and any approved concessions live apart from the client-facing document and are never shown to the prospect.

  3. Send as normal

    Deliver the proposal the way you always do. The assistant becomes available on the page when a prospect opens a link you have issued.

  4. Follow the conversation

    Questions, objections and hesitations surface in your dashboard as the prospect works through the document, so your next call starts informed.

Capabilities

What the assistant does.

Every capability below is built around one constraint: the assistant may only work within what you have approved. It is a sales presence, not a negotiator with a free hand.

Intelligent, interactive proposals

A proposal that answers back. Instead of a static PDF, the prospect can interrogate the scope, the sequence and the assumptions behind the number.

Proposal-specific assistants

Each assistant belongs to one proposal. It knows that document and nothing else, so it cannot accidentally mix one client’s commercial terms into another’s conversation.

Objection handling within boundaries

Price pushback, timing concerns and comparisons get a considered response from approved material. Where an answer would cross a boundary, the assistant declines and hands the conversation back to you.

Your branding, front to back

Name, subtitle, accent colour and vendor branding are configurable per proposal. By default the prospect sees your firm, not a third-party product badge.

Works with existing proposals

Add it to a proposal you have already written rather than rebuilding it in a new format. Your document structure and house style stay yours.

Engagement and conversation intelligence

See what was asked, what stalled, and which sections drew the most attention. Observed activity and inferred signals are reported separately, so an estimate is never presented as a measurement.

Acceptance and payment

The prospect can accept the proposal on the page, with a timestamp, the document version and a signature of the exact document they agreed to. A payment step can follow sign-off where you use one.

Internal terms stay internal

Price floors, discount ceilings and negotiation notes are stored apart from the client-facing proposal. There is no path by which the prospect’s page can read them.

Control you keep

Revoke a link and the live conversation stops on the next message. Links can be scoped to one domain and given an expiry. Nothing is permanent unless you leave it that way.
