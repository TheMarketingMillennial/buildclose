Privacy notice

This notice covers this website only — buildclose.app. It describes the one thing the site collects.

What this website collects

The single data entry point on this site is the “Request a demo” form. If you submit it, we receive:

  • your name
  • your company name
  • your work email address
  • the industry you select, if you select one
  • any optional note you choose to write

Submitting the form is the only way this site sends us anything. Nothing is collected if you only read the page.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies and no local storage.
  • No analytics, no tag manager, no session recording, no heatmaps.
  • No advertising or tracking pixels of any kind.
  • No third-party scripts. The page loads nothing from any other domain — that is why its Content-Security-Policy allows only its own origin.
  • No fonts from a CDN. Typefaces are system fonts, so no font request leaves your browser.

Why we hold it

Only to arrange and conduct the walkthrough you asked for, and to reply to you about it. Not for a mailing list, not for advertising, and not sold or rented to anyone.

Where it goes

Form submissions are stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, and are visible to the BuildClose team. We do not pass them to any other service.

How long we keep it

Demo enquiries are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, and removed on request. If you ask us to delete your enquiry, we will.

Your choices

Email hello@buildclose.app to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. You do not need to give a reason.

The product, not this site

Once you are a customer, proposal data, conversations and acceptance records are handled under a separate agreement between your firm and BuildClose. This page does not describe that arrangement.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it.
